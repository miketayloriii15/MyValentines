Heartfelt Note

I've learned so much from you. You challenge me, inspire me, and, yes, sometimes you drive me crazy. But even in those moments, I wouldn’t trade you for the world. What we have is real. It’s not perfect, but it's ours. And that means more to me than anything. No matter what life throws our way, my love for you will never waver. It is endless, unbreakable, and forever yours. I will always choose you. Over and over again. In this life and every life after.

With all my heart, always and forever
